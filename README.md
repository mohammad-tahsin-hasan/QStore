# QStore

**Scan • Store • Generate** — A clean, fast QR code manager for Android.

QStore lets you scan, organize, generate, share, and cloud-sync QR codes in one place.

### How to Install
1. Download the `.apk` file from the Google Drive link above.
2. Open the downloaded file on your Android device (Android 7.0+).
3. If prompted, allow **Install from unknown sources** for your browser or file manager, then tap **Install**.

## Features

- **Smart QR Scanner**
  - Live camera scanning and **Scan from Gallery** support.
  - Automatically detects QR types and suggests a title before saving.
- **Organized Store**
  - Categorizes QR codes into **Wi-Fi**, **Contact**, **Link**, **Email**, **Phone / SMS**, **Location**, and **Text**.
  - Instant search and category filter chips.
  - Tap any saved QR code to display it with automatic screen brightness boost for reliable scanning.
  - **One-tap actions:** Connect to Wi-Fi networks, save contacts to your phone, copy fields, **Share** as PNG, or **Download** to your gallery.
- **QR Generator**
  - Dedicated forms for each category so you can create and save structured QR codes in seconds.
- **Cloud Sync & Guest Mode**
  - Sign in with **Google** to back up and sync your QR codes in real time across your devices.
  - Or use **Guest Mode** to keep everything stored locally on your device.
- **Backup & Restore**
  - Export and import your QR library as a `.json` backup anytime.

## Tech Stack

- **Language & UI:** Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Material 3 (Light & Dark themes)
- **Scanning & Generation:** CameraX, Google ML Kit Barcode Scanning, ZXing
- **Auth & Cloud Sync:** Google Sign-In, Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore
- **Compatibility:** Android 7.0 (API 24) and above
